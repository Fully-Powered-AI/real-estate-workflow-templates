# Fully Powered Workflow Templates: Real Estate

Every property's facts straight, every time. These workflow templates are written for the people who manage and market property. They prepare the administrative checking and reporting from the records you supply. They value nothing, advise on no transaction and interpret no lease.

This repository holds 6 workflow templates for property managers, brokers and their operations staff. Each one is a single piece of work with its own folder: what it is for and who starts it, the complete template an AI assistant follows, a worked example with invented data, what to change for your company, what has been checked, and its sources.

## The workflow templates

- **[Listing copy fact check](listing-copy-fact-check/)**: The listing agent or marketing lead gets draft listing copy in which every statement is tied to the property sheet, a measurement or a photo, with unsupported descriptions marked and kept out. The listing broker approves; the designated reviewer checks advertising requirements.
- **[Property diligence index](property-diligence-index/)**: The transaction lead gets a diligence room in order: every document indexed by workstream, every checklist item and request tracked, and every open finding tied to the report or request that raised it. Specialists judge the findings; nothing is valued or sent.
- **[Property owner report](property-owner-report/)**: A property manager gets the month's owner report drafted from the statements and logs they supply: occupancy, collections, open maintenance and the decisions the owner is asked to make, each statement traced to its source and each gap shown.
- **[Rent roll to lease abstract match](rent-roll-to-lease-abstract-match/)**: Your property accountant gets the rent roll checked against the verified lease abstracts and amendments, unit by unit: rent, area, commencement and expiration, with every difference listed for review. It compares supplied fields only and corrects nothing.
- **[Unit turnover readiness checklist](unit-turnover-readiness-checklist/)**: Between one tenant and the next, the property manager gets one page for the unit: every recorded repair, the cleaning, keys, utilities and vendor confirmations still open against the approved move-in date, each gap written as a question. Nothing is sent.
- **[Vendor insurance certificate check](vendor-insurance-certificate-check/)**: For a property manager collecting insurance certificates from service vendors: each certificate's fields set beside the requirements the team already approved, every stated expiration date listed, and draft requests for what is missing. The manager reviews gaps with the insurance adviser before accepting anything.

## How to use one

Open a folder and start with its README. Every folder stands alone, so copy the one you want and leave the rest. The templates are written for ChatGPT, Claude, Copilot and Gemini: paste the instruction block and the output format in as custom instructions, or load the folder in a product that reads `SKILL.md` packages.

To take the whole collection, press **Use this template** at the top of this page: it copies every folder into a repository in your own account, yours to change.

In every template a person starts the work, supplies the inputs and decides. The template prepares and checks. Anything that sends, posts, pays or changes a record waits for a person to approve it.

## What SKILL.md is

Each folder holds a file named `SKILL.md`. It is the technical file: the same instructions and output format as the template, in the packaged form that AI products which load instructions from a folder read. It is generated from `TEMPLATE.md`, so the two always agree. The workflow template is the whole folder; `SKILL.md` is one way of loading it. If your AI product does not read that format, use `TEMPLATE.md`.

## What "checked" means here

Each folder says exactly what was checked and when: who wrote it, that its sources and rights were reviewed, the date of its editorial review, and whether it has been tried on its invented example in a named AI product. Nothing is described as tested for a client unless it was. Test a template on your own past work before your team relies on it; each one comes with acceptance tests for that.
