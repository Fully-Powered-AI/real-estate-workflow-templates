# Listing copy fact check: an invented example

*Illustrative example with invented data.* The property, the people and every figure below are invented. The brokerage and the measurer appear only as letters.

**Setting.** The listing agent at Brokerage X is preparing a rental listing for Unit 4C at an invented building, Fernhollow Lofts. The run date is 12 November 2026. The listing broker is Broker A and the designated reviewer is Reviewer A.

- Fact: Bedrooms. Value: 2. Source: Property sheet.

**Measurements**

- Space: Living room. Area: 252. Unit: sq ft. Basis: Measured by Measurer A, 3 November 2026.

**Amenity list**

- Amenity: Bike storage. Status: Confirmed. Note: Ground floor.
- Amenity: Fitness room. Status: Planned. Note: Under renovation, reopening date not set.
- Amenity: Concierge. Status: Unconfirmed. Note: Not confirmed by the building.

**Photos** (listed by label only; no images are attached, so none is viewed)

- P4. Shows: Rooftop terrace with seating.

**Advertising guidelines** (the firm's list)

- G1: No statement about the kind of person who should or would like to live in the property.
- G2: Distances and commute times need a source.
- G3: Rent is shown with its terms as on the sheet.
- G4: Photos must show the unit as it is.

**The listing agent's draft**

"Sun-drenched, luxury 2-bedroom loft ideal for a busy couple. 912 sq ft of open living with hardwood floors throughout. Steps from the subway. In-unit laundry, a gym, concierge service and a stunning rooftop terrace. Pet friendly. Available now at $2,850."

Channel: listing description. Length limit: 90 words.

**4. HELD OUT OF THE COPY, AND WHAT WOULD SETTLE IT**

1. S1, S2, S7, S13: descriptive words with no source. A source for each (for example a floor plan for "open living"), or the broker accepts their absence.
2. S5: a claim about the kind of person the unit suits. The broker decides; Reviewer A sees it in section 5.
3. S9: the station's name and a distance or walking time from a source the broker accepts.
4. S11: the fitness room is Planned and under renovation with no reopening date. The broker decides whether to drop it or add it once a date exists.
5. S12: confirmation from the building that a concierge exists, and what it covers.
6. S15: the owner's pet policy, supplied in writing.

**5. FOR THE DESIGNATED REVIEWER**

1. S5: touches G1. A claim about who the unit suits; not in the copy.
2. S9: touches G2. A distance claim with no source; not in the copy.
3. S17: touches G3. Rent appears as $2,850 per month, the only terms on the sheet; no lease length was supplied.
4. P1 to P4: touch G4. Listed only, not viewed. No photo is used in the copy. By label, P1 to P3 show rooms of the unit; P4 shows the shared rooftop terrace, outside the unit.
This review gives no opinion on whether any item meets an advertising requirement.
